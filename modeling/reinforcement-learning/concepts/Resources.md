# Resources

- https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/10-703/textbook/BarsutoSutworkon.pdf - Sutton & Barto, "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction" (2nd edition). The standard textbook covering MDPs, Bellman equations, dynamic programming, and exploration.
- https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/ - OpenAI Spinning Up. Practical introduction to RL concepts including key equations, policy types, and algorithm taxonomy.
- https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-02-19-rl-overview/ - Lilian Weng, "A (Long) Peek into Reinforcement Learning." Comprehensive overview of RL fundamentals including MDPs, value functions, and Bellman equations.
- https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs234/ - Stanford CS234: Reinforcement Learning. Lecture materials covering MDP formulation, dynamic programming, and exploration.
- https://www.davidsilver.uk/teaching/ - David Silver's RL Course (UCL/DeepMind). Lecture slides and videos covering MDPs, Bellman equations, dynamic programming, and model-free methods.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12560 - Burda et al. (2019), "Exploration by Random Network Distillation." Introduces RND for exploration in sparse-reward environments.
- https://pathak22.github.io/noreward-rl/ - Pathak et al. (2017), "Curiosity-driven Exploration by Self-Supervised Prediction." Introduces the Intrinsic Curiosity Module.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461 - Mnih et al. (2016), "Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning." Introduces A3C and discusses on-policy exploration.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347 - Schulman et al. (2017), "Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms." PPO algorithm with entropy bonus for exploration.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290 - Haarnoja et al. (2018), "Soft Actor-Critic." Maximum entropy RL framework.
