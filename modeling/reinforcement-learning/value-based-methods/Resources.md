# Value-Based Methods Resources

## Original Papers

- [Learning from Delayed Rewards (Watkins, 1989) - PhD Thesis introducing Q-Learning](https://www.cs.rhul.ac.uk/~chrisw/new_thesis.pdf)
- [Q-Learning (Watkins & Dayan, 1992) - Convergence proof](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00992698)
- [Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (Mnih et al., 2013) - DQN preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5602)
- [Human-level Control through Deep Reinforcement Learning (Mnih et al., 2015) - DQN Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236)
- [Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning (van Hasselt et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461)
- [Dueling Network Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning (Wang et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06581)
- [Prioritized Experience Replay (Schaul et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05952)
- [Rainbow: Combining Improvements in Deep Reinforcement Learning (Hessel et al., 2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02298)
