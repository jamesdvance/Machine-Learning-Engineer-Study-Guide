# Q-Learning Resources

## Original Papers

- [Learning from Delayed Rewards (Watkins, 1989) - PhD Thesis introducing Q-Learning](https://www.cs.rhul.ac.uk/~chrisw/new_thesis.pdf)
- [Q-Learning (Watkins & Dayan, 1992) - Convergence proof for Q-Learning](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00992698)
- [Double Q-Learning (van Hasselt, 2010) - Addressing maximization bias](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2010/hash/091d584fced301b442654dd8c23b3fc9-Abstract.html)
- [Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (Mnih et al., 2013) - DQN preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5602)
- [Human-level Control through Deep Reinforcement Learning (Mnih et al., 2015) - DQN in Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236)
