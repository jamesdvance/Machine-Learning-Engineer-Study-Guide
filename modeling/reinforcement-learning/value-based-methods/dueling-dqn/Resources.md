# Resources

## Papers

- [Dueling Network Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning (Wang et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06581) - Original paper introducing the dueling architecture
- [Rainbow: Combining Improvements in Deep Reinforcement Learning (Hessel et al., 2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02298) - Combines dueling with five other DQN improvements
- [Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning (van Hasselt et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461) - Double DQN, commonly combined with dueling
- [Prioritized Experience Replay (Schaul et al., 2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05952) - Another complementary improvement often used with dueling
- [Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning (Mnih et al., 2015)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236) - Original DQN paper

## Implementations

- [CleanRL Dueling DQN](https://github.com/vwxyzjn/cleanrl) - Single-file implementations of RL algorithms including DQN variants
- [Stable-Baselines3 DQN](https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/dqn.html) - Production-quality DQN with dueling support
- [PyTorch DQN Tutorial](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/intermediate/reinforcement_q_learning.html) - Official PyTorch tutorial for DQN (adaptable to dueling)
