# Resources

## Papers

- [Simple Statistical Gradient-Following Algorithms for Connectionist Reinforcement Learning (Williams, 1992)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00992696) - The original REINFORCE paper.
- [Policy Gradient Methods for Reinforcement Learning with Function Approximation (Sutton et al., 1999)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/1999/hash/464d828b85b0bed98e80ade0a5c43b0f-Abstract.html) - The policy gradient theorem.
- [High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation (Schulman et al., 2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438) - GAE, connecting REINFORCE baselines to Actor-Critic.

## Textbooks

- Sutton and Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, 2nd Edition, Chapter 13 (Policy Gradient Methods) - https://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book.html

## Tutorials and Articles

- [OpenAI Spinning Up: Policy Gradient](https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/spinningup/rl_intro3.html) - Accessible introduction to policy gradients and REINFORCE.
- [Lilian Weng: Policy Gradient Algorithms](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-04-08-policy-gradient/) - Comprehensive survey from REINFORCE to PPO.

## Implementations

- [CleanRL REINFORCE](https://docs.cleanrl.dev/) - Single-file, well-documented RL implementations.
- [PyTorch Official REINFORCE Example](https://github.com/pytorch/examples/blob/main/reinforcement_learning/reinforce.py) - Minimal reference implementation.
